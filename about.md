I'm Pablo Silva. I run Global Business Operations for Paramount Skydance in Latin America. The job sits where media, technology and operations meet: large-scale business transformation, M&A integration and separation work, and the AI and systems modernization that comes with running a region through a lot of change. The scorecard is savings, cost avoidance, synergies and efficiencies, delivered without breaking what already works.

I've spent 25-plus years in media and technology, most of it at the global level. Before Paramount I worked at [Fox International Channels](https://www.fox.com/) through the [Disney](https://www.disney.com/) acquisition, at [Brightcove](https://www.brightcove.com/), at [Toolbox](https://toolboxtve.com/en/) and at [Turner](https://www.warnerbrosdiscovery.com/), with long assignments in São Paulo, Buenos Aires and London. That adds up to a lot of integrations, carve-outs, platform migrations and launches across the US, Latin America and Europe, which is the part of the work I find most interesting: taking something complicated and making it run. The full record is on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/pablosilva/).

AI is now a core part of how I run operations, and I spend a good share of my time on the question most teams are still working through: where it belongs in a workflow, where it doesn't and what it takes to move an organization from pilots to daily use. I'm active in the Miami AI community through [DeepStation](https://deepstation.ai) and the local [Claude](https://claude.ai)/[Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com) chapter, among others, and I write here about what works in practice rather than in demos.

Outside the day job I ship. [Ghost Gym Gear](https://gggear.app) is a community app for Ghost Gym Miami built around a catalog of vintage strength equipment, and the place where I put the current generation of AI coding tools to work on a real product. [Hydrate Stupid](https://hydratestupid.com) is a small hardware brand that started with a gallon water jug tether: I loved the jug and hated the cap, so I designed a better way to carry it.

I was born and raised in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, and I live in Coral Gables, FL, with my wife and son. I speak English, Portuguese and Spanish, in whatever order the room needs.

Silvasonic is where I think out loud. Opinions are mine. You can also find me on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/silvasonic) and [GitHub](https://github.com/silvasonic).
