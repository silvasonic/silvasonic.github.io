🏋️ Ghost Gym Gear is a community project: a catalog of the rare, old-school and vintage iron at [Ghost Gym Miami](https://ghostgymmiami.com). Every machine gets photos, its story and its specs.

**Install**

🍎 **iPhone:** install TestFlight from <https://apps.apple.com/app/testflight/id899247664>. Then open <https://testflight.apple.com/join/aAXp9aCk> and tap **Accept**, then **Install**.

🤖 **Android:** tap **Join** here: <https://groups.google.com/g/gggear-app>. Then tap **Become a tester** here: <https://play.google.com/apps/testing/app.gggear>, and install from Play. **Please keep the app installed for at least 2 weeks.** Google needs 12 testers for 14 days before the app can go public.

**What to try**

- Browse, search and filter machines, then share one
- Add the **Featured machine** widget to your home screen
- Turn on **announcements** when the app asks (you'll get news about new equipment, meets and events)

**Found a bug?**

On iPhone, take a screenshot and tap **Share Beta Feedback** in TestFlight. On Android, email <support@gggear.app>. Screenshots help a lot.

**The fine print**

It's free, with no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no ads and no account. Nothing tracks you, and notifications are optional.

Thanks for helping! 🙏
